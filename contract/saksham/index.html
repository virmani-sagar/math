2. Purpose and Nature of Engagement

The Organisation engages the Freelancer in a dual professional capacity as a Freelance Mathematics Trainer and Programme Manager for the Math education services offered through math.eduspace.work.

The engagement covers academic delivery, student coordination, programme administration and related responsibilities required for the effective operation of the mathematics training programme.

The Freelancer agrees to perform the assigned services responsibly, professionally and in accordance with the Organisation's academic standards, programme guidelines and reasonable operational instructions.

3. Role and Responsibilities

3.1 Mathematics Trainer

The Freelancer shall be responsible for:

  • Conducting live, personalised one-to-one online mathematics classes for assigned students.
  • Providing mathematics training across the school grades assigned by the Organisation.
  • Following the relevant academic curriculum, including US Common Core where applicable to the assigned programme.
  • Preparing lesson plans, exercises, practice materials and revision activities.
  • Explaining mathematical concepts in an age-appropriate and understandable manner.
  • Monitoring student participation, understanding and academic progress.
  • Conducting assessments and providing constructive feedback.
  • Maintaining accurate attendance and class-completion records.
  • Providing academic updates to parents or guardians through approved communication channels.
  • Supporting additional academic sessions when required and mutually agreed.

3.2 Programme Manager – Math

The Freelancer shall also assist in:

  • Coordinating the delivery of assigned mathematics programmes.
  • Managing student schedules and class coordination.
  • Communicating with parents or guardians regarding class schedules and academic matters.
  • Monitoring attendance, class completion and programme continuity.
  • Maintaining student progress records and programme-related documentation.
  • Coordinating student onboarding and academic requirements.
  • Reporting operational concerns, complaints and scheduling issues to the Organisation.
  • Supporting the implementation of academic policies and programme guidelines.
  • Providing periodic updates regarding programme activities and student progress.

3.3 General Professional Responsibilities

  • Maintain punctuality, reliability and professional conduct.
  • Communicate any planned unavailability in advance.
  • Protect the reputation and interests of the Organisation.
  • Follow the Organisation's approved communication and operational procedures.
  • Obtain approval before making commitments outside the assigned responsibilities.

4. Programme Delivery and Scheduling

The Freelancer shall deliver classes according to the schedule agreed with the Organisation and the relevant parents or guardians.

  • Classes shall generally be conducted through live online sessions.
  • Class frequency, duration and scheduling shall follow the applicable student programme or proposal.
  • Any changes to confirmed schedules shall be coordinated with the Organisation and affected parents or guardians.
  • Missed, rescheduled or additional classes shall be handled according to the applicable programme arrangements.
  • The Freelancer shall maintain appropriate records of classes conducted and pending sessions.

The Freelancer shall not independently change a student's agreed programme structure, class commitments or fees without the Organisation's approval.

5. Compensation and Payment Terms

The Freelancer's compensation for training, programme management and any additional assigned responsibilities shall be as discussed and mutually agreed between the Parties.

  • Compensation: As discussed and mutually agreed.
  • Payment cycle: As mutually agreed.
  • Payment method: As discussed and mutually agreed.
  • Additional assignments: Compensation, where applicable, shall be mutually agreed before the work is undertaken.

Any revision or change to the agreed compensation arrangement shall require mutual confirmation in writing.

The Freelancer shall not independently collect student programme fees, retain payments, offer unauthorised discounts or make financial commitments on behalf of the Organisation.

Any applicable tax obligations shall be handled by the respective Parties in accordance with applicable law.

6. Student Enrolment and Programme Administration

Student enrolment, programme pricing, fee structures, scholarships, discounts, promotional offers and official payment arrangements shall remain under the authority of the Organisation.

The Freelancer shall:

  • Follow the student programme and fee arrangements communicated by the Organisation.
  • Provide accurate academic and scheduling information to parents.
  • Refer payment-related queries to the Organisation's authorised contact.
  • Not make independent promises regarding discounts, refunds, scholarships or financial benefits.
  • Not redirect students or parents to personal payment accounts or unauthorised services.

7. Academic Quality and Performance

The Freelancer shall maintain appropriate teaching quality and programme standards.

Performance may be reviewed on the basis of:

  • Teaching quality and preparation.
  • Class punctuality and consistency.
  • Student engagement and understanding.
  • Completion of agreed classes.
  • Student progress and assessment records.
  • Parent communication and feedback.
  • Accuracy of programme documentation.
  • Compliance with the Organisation's policies.

The Freelancer acknowledges that academic results depend on several factors, including student participation, practice, attendance and individual learning requirements. No particular examination score or academic outcome is guaranteed.

8. Confidentiality and Data Protection

During the engagement, the Freelancer may have access to confidential Organisation and student information. Such information includes, but is not limited to:

  • Student and parent contact details.
  • Student academic records, assessments and progress reports.
  • Programme fees, payment information and business arrangements.
  • Internal documents, training resources and operational processes.
  • Login credentials, administrative access and unpublished website information.
  • Business plans, proposals and other non-public information.

The Freelancer agrees to:

  • Keep confidential information secure and private.
  • Use such information only for authorised programme responsibilities.
  • Not disclose student or parent information to unauthorised persons.
  • Not use student contact details for personal or unrelated commercial purposes.
  • Protect account credentials and access to Organisation systems.
  • Promptly report suspected data loss, unauthorised access or disclosure.
  • Return or securely delete confidential information when the engagement ends, subject to applicable legal requirements.

These confidentiality obligations shall continue after termination of this Agreement.

9. Intellectual Property and Work Ownership

The Organisation's brand identity, website, logos, programme structure, student database, business documents, templates and other Organisation-provided materials shall remain the property of the Organisation or their respective rights holders.

Materials specifically commissioned by the Organisation and created by the Freelancer for the assigned programme shall be owned by the Organisation upon payment of the agreed compensation, to the extent permitted by law.

Pre-existing materials independently owned by the Freelancer shall remain the Freelancer's property unless otherwise agreed in writing.

The Freelancer shall not commercially distribute confidential Organisation materials or student-specific learning records without authorisation.

10. Brand Representation and Authority

The Freelancer may identify themselves as an authorised Freelance Mathematics Trainer and Programme Manager associated with the Math programme during the period of engagement.

The Freelancer shall not:

  • Represent themselves as the owner or founder of OPENED LABS.
  • Claim ownership of the Organisation's website, brand, domain or business identity.
  • Make unauthorised business, financial or contractual commitments.
  • Publish official announcements or promotional material without required approval.
  • Use the Organisation's name or branding for unrelated activities.

11. Student Safety and Professional Conduct

The Freelancer acknowledges that the programme may involve children and school-age students.

The Freelancer shall:

  • Maintain respectful, appropriate and professional communication.
  • Maintain appropriate professional boundaries with students.
  • Use approved platforms and channels for classes and programme communication.
  • Keep parents or guardians appropriately informed.
  • Not request unnecessary personal or sensitive information from students.
  • Not record, publish or distribute student images, voices or class sessions without the required authorisation.
  • Report safeguarding concerns or inappropriate conduct to the Organisation promptly.
  • Follow applicable child-safety and data-protection requirements.

12. Conflict of Interest and Student Relationships

The Freelancer may undertake other professional or freelance assignments, provided such activities do not interfere with agreed responsibilities or misuse the Organisation's confidential information.

The Freelancer shall not:

  • Divert students introduced through the Organisation into unauthorised private paid arrangements.
  • Use Organisation student or parent contact lists for personal commercial activities.
  • Misrepresent the Organisation's services, pricing or programme arrangements.
  • Use confidential business information to obtain an unfair commercial advantage.

Nothing in this Agreement shall prevent the Freelancer from undertaking lawful independent professional work, subject to the confidentiality and other obligations stated herein.

13. Independent Freelance Relationship

The Parties expressly acknowledge that this engagement is a freelance independent-contractor arrangement and does not, by itself, create an employer-employee, partnership or joint-venture relationship.

  • The Freelancer shall perform the agreed services as an independent professional.
  • The Freelancer shall not be treated as a permanent, full-time or part-time employee solely by virtue of this Agreement.
  • The Freelancer shall be responsible for their own professional expenses unless otherwise agreed.
  • The Freelancer shall not be entitled to employee benefits solely by virtue of this freelance engagement, subject to applicable law.
  • The Freelancer shall have no authority to bind the Organisation contractually or financially without written authorisation.

The actual nature of the working relationship shall be determined in accordance with applicable law, regardless of the description used in this Agreement.

14. Term and Termination

This Agreement shall commence from the date of acceptance and shall continue until terminated by either Party in accordance with this section.

Either Party may end the engagement by providing reasonable prior written notice, with the notice period to be mutually agreed.

The Organisation may suspend assigned responsibilities or terminate the engagement in the event of a serious breach, including:

  • Misuse or unauthorised disclosure of confidential information.
  • Misuse of student or parent information.
  • Unauthorised collection or diversion of student payments.
  • Serious misconduct or breach of student-safety requirements.
  • Fraudulent representation or misuse of Organisation branding.
  • Repeated failure to fulfil agreed responsibilities.

Upon termination:

  • The Freelancer shall reasonably assist with the handover of pending programme responsibilities.
  • Outstanding undisputed compensation for services properly delivered shall be settled as mutually agreed and subject to applicable law.
  • Organisation documents, materials and access credentials shall be returned or appropriately removed.
  • The Freelancer shall cease representing themselves as an active representative of the Organisation.
  • Confidentiality and other obligations intended to continue after termination shall remain effective.

15. Policies and Operational Guidelines

The Freelancer agrees to follow the relevant academic, student communication, programme administration, privacy and operational guidelines communicated by the Organisation from time to time.

Where an operational instruction conflicts with this Agreement, the Parties shall clarify the matter and record any agreed contractual amendment in writing.

16. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, subject to applicable legal requirements.

The Parties shall first attempt to resolve any disagreement or dispute through good-faith discussion. If the matter cannot be resolved amicably, it may be referred to a court or other competent forum having jurisdiction under applicable law.

17. Amendments and General Terms

  • Any change to the terms of this Agreement shall be mutually agreed and recorded in writing.
  • Operational details may be communicated through authorised written communication channels.
  • This Agreement represents the understanding between the Parties concerning the freelance engagement.
  • If any provision is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue to apply to the extent permitted by law.
  • Both Parties confirm that they have had the opportunity to read and understand the Agreement before accepting.
